# M07 — Responsible AI Visual Practice

**Competency:** AV-08 · **Status:** Pilot draft

**Understand:** Identify provenance, privacy, consent, representational harm, misleading imagery and uncertainty in a scenario. Explain why a plausible image is not evidence of a real event.

**Apply task:** Complete a risk/provenance log for a hypothetical marketing image: source of inputs, permissions/unknowns, intended use, sensitive features, disclosure decision, human reviewer and unresolved risks.

**Create task:** Respond to a new client request involving a realistic synthetic person and a brand endorsement. Propose safer creative alternatives, escalation and disclosure/approval process; justify trade-offs.

**Evidence:** scenario annotations, provenance log, mitigation memo and reviewer feedback. **Assessment:** [pilot rubric](../../framework/pilot-assessment-rubric-v0.1.md).

**Sources:** [UNESCO AI competency framework](https://doi.org/10.54675/JKJB9835), [NIST GenAI Profile](https://doi.org/10.6028/NIST.AI.600-1). This is not jurisdiction-specific legal advice.
