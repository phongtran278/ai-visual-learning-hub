# M02 — Composition & Negative Space

**Competency:** AV-02 · **Status:** Pilot draft · **Stages:** Understand → Apply → Create

**Evidence:** [Google Gemini image generation documentation](https://ai.google.dev/gemini-api/docs/image-generation) documents image-generation direction and capabilities; [UNESCO framework](https://doi.org/10.54675/JKJB9835) supplies adapted progression. Neither validates this rubric.

## Understand
Compare two example editorial images or instructor-provided sketches. Mark subject hierarchy, camera angle, framing, depth, alignment and usable headline-safe space. Explain why empty space can be functional, not wasted.

## Apply
**Provided brief:** vertical 4:5 campaign; five people with distinguishable silhouettes; primary figure near center; text-safe area at upper left; realistic floor contact. Write an explicit composition instruction, generate one or more images and annotate whether each constraint holds. Revise a single failed constraint while attempting to preserve others.

## Create
**Transfer brief:** a different brand requests an elegant 16:9 LED backdrop, with central region intentionally unobstructed for photography. Propose two layouts; select one based on viewing context and obstruction risk. Produce or sketch the chosen direction, explain trade-offs and record revisions.

## Assessment evidence
- Understand: correct annotations and explanation.
- Apply: declared constraints, prompt/settings, images, checklist and correction attempt.
- Create: two alternative layouts, chosen solution and justification.

Score each stage 0/1/2 using [pilot rubric](../../framework/pilot-assessment-rubric-v0.1.md). Do not infer cross-model guarantees from a single generation.

## Known failure modes
Crowded negative space; accidental cropping; inconsistent camera/floor geometry; prompts that specify composition but outputs ignore it. Document failures rather than deleting them.
