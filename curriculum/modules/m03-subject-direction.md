# M03 — Subject Count, Pose & Interaction

**Competency:** AV-03 · **Status:** Pilot draft

**Understand:** Annotate a sample visual brief: exact subject count, role hierarchy, pose differences, interpersonal spacing, physical contact, and common anatomy errors.

**Apply task:** Direct **exactly five** professionals in a 4:5 editorial scene, with one leader and four supporting subjects, each with a distinct pose. Record count, spacing and anatomy failures; make one targeted correction. The objective is the *direction and diagnosis process*, not a promise the model always complies.

**Create task:** For a novel brief, propose and justify subject roles, arrangement and camera treatment; generate or storyboard and critique.

**Evidence:** brief, prompts, all candidate images, before/after comparison, count/pose checklist, rationale, model/version. **Assessment:** 0/1/2 per stage using [rubric](../../framework/pilot-assessment-rubric-v0.1.md).

**Sources:** [OpenAI Images](https://help.openai.com/en/articles/11084440-images-in-chatgpt); [Google Gemini image guide](https://ai.google.dev/gemini-api/docs/image-generation). Product documentation does not establish consistent multi-person accuracy.
