This is an evidence-informed **prototype curriculum**, not a tested learning intervention, industry standard, or UNESCO-accredited program. Learners may progress differently in each competency. AV-07 Production Readiness remains provisional and outside the pilot core. Future modules depend on designer interviews, learning evidence and rubric testing.
