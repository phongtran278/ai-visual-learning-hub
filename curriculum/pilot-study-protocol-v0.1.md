# Pilot Study Protocol v0.1

**Not yet executed.** This is a preregistration-style plan, not research results.

## Questions
1. Can independent reviewers distinguish Understand, Apply and Create in submitted artifacts?
2. Which competencies produce clear, reliable evidence?
3. Which model limitations interfere with assessment?
4. Do learners improve on a comparable transfer task after the lessons? (Exploratory; not causal without controls.)

## Procedure
- Recruit a small voluntary cohort with diverse design experience; disclose study purpose and privacy terms.
- Collect baseline artifact from a standard brief, prior experience and tool/version.
- Teach M01–M07 with task-level artifacts and feedback.
- Collect a different but comparable transfer brief, revision log and learner reflection.
- Have two independent reviewers score a subset blind where feasible.
- Report task completion, score distributions, reviewer disagreements, missing data, and failure cases.
- Do not publish identifiable learner images, prompts containing secrets, or raw personal data without informed permission.

## Interpretation
No randomized comparison group means improvement cannot be attributed uniquely to the curriculum. Different models, prior expertise and prompt budgets confound outcomes. State sample size and attrition. Revise rubrics after observing disagreement.

## Outputs
Pilot report, anonymized aggregates, updated rubric, prioritized curriculum changes and documented evidence limitations.
