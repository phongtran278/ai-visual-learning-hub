**Status:** Research-informed **pilot**, not validated training or UNESCO certification. **Date:** 2026-10-22.

**Adaptation rule:** Understand → Apply → Create are adapted level labels from UNESCO's *AI competency framework for students* (2024). The seven pilot domains and exercises are the Hub's own specializations; **Create does not mean merely generating an image**.

## Pilot sequence

| Module | Hub competency | Understand: observable learning outcome | Apply: observable outcome | Create: observable outcome | Evidence and caveat |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| M01 | AV-01 Brief translation | Identify audience, purpose, constraints, acceptance criteria | Rewrite a supplied brief into an explicit checklist | Resolve conflicting constraints in a new brief and justify choices | SRC-005/006; model-independent skill hypothesis |
| M02 | AV-02 Composition | Explain framing, hierarchy and negative space | Produce a composition meeting placement criteria | Develop two compositions for a new campaign, choose and defend one | SRC-004; visual design theory pending |
| M03 | AV-03 Subject direction | Explain count, pose and interactions | Correct subject count and pose against a brief | Art-direct a new ensemble with distinct roles | SRC-003/004; success varies by model |
| M04 | AV-04 Light/material/style | Recognize light direction, contrast and material cues | Match a supplied style specification | Create coherent treatment for two related assets | SRC-004; visual design literature pending |
| M05 | AV-05 Reference/edit | Explain reference, edit and reproduction differences | Edit selected detail and document unintended changes | Plan rights-aware multi-step reference/edit workflow | SRC-003/004; product capability varies |
| M06 | AV-06 Critique/iteration | Identify objective mismatches | Make targeted revision preserving other constraints | Design and transfer a repeatable critique loop | SRC-005/006, AC-001 (indirect) |
| M07 | AV-08 Responsible practice | Identify consent/provenance/risk issues | Log source, permission, uncertainty and disclosure | Justify mitigation decisions in a client scenario | SRC-001/002; not legal advice |

**AV-07 Production readiness** remains provisional; do not award pilot mastery for it.

## Every module uses the same lesson format
1. **Why:** problem scenario and evidence card (what sources support, what they do not).
2. **Understand:** compare/annotate and explain.
3. **Apply:** execute a constrained brief, capture settings and output.
4. **Create:** transfer to a novel brief, justify decisions and alternatives.
5. **Reflect:** critique, risks, uncertainty, and next experiment.

## Assessment and progression
- Score each criterion **0 / 1 / 2** (not demonstrated / partial / demonstrated). See [pilot rubric](../framework/pilot-assessment-rubric-v0.1.md).
- Capture **brief, prompt, model/product version, settings if available, outputs (including failed attempts), edit log, reflection and assessor notes**.
- Do not equate one good image with competency. Record independent assessments where possible.
- Allow learner-specific profiles by domain; three stages are **not** a fixed age or time progression.
- Completion means finishing the pilot tasks, **not** UNESCO endorsement or certified proficiency.

## Pilot study proposal (not yet run)
Recruit consenting designers of varied experience. Collect a baseline task, guided learning, and a comparable new transfer task. Use at least two independent assessors for a sample of work. Track completion, rubric agreement, common failure modes and learner feedback. Report limitations and do not overclaim causality.
