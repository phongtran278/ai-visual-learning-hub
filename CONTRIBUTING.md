# Contributing

Welcome! This is a nonprofit-oriented, evidence-informed educational project.

## Propose a change
Open an issue describing the learning problem, the proposed claim, the supporting sources, and the scope/limitations. Prefer one claim per change.

## Research contributions
Provide stable URLs/DOIs, publication and access dates, the relevant section, platform/model applicability, and competing evidence. Do not copy copyrighted documents into the repo.

## Experiments
Include exact prompts, model and date, test protocol, all results (not only the best image), rubric and limitations. Respect privacy and rights to submitted images.

## Review
All content is provisional until reviewed. Disclose potential conflicts of interest. Corrections are welcome. Do not submit learner personal data, credentials, API keys, or private images.

## Licensing
Licenses for code and educational content are under review; no blanket reuse license is granted yet.
