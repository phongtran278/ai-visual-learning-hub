# Research & Validation Protocol — v0.1

## Evidence classes
- **A — Scholarly:** peer-reviewed research or established design/learning theory, with context and limitations.
- **B — Official:** vendor or standards-body documentation, versioned and scoped.
- **C — Experimental:** reproducible Hub tests, including negative results.
- **D — Hypothesis:** plausible but not yet independently verified.

These classes are **types**, not a numerical quality ranking. Multiple classes can support one claim.

## Claim workflow
1. Write an atomic, falsifiable claim.
2. Link exact supporting passages or research findings; note whether they support or merely motivate the claim.
3. Record platform/model, dates, conditions and caveats.
4. Design an assessment or experiment where appropriate.
5. Mark status: proposed / documented / tested / contradicted / superseded.
6. Re-review when a model or product changes.

## Experiment template
Question; hypothesis; model/version; interface; test date; reference assets; exact prompt; parameter/settings if known; number of trials; randomization/order; predefined rubric; assessor protocol; raw outcomes; failure cases; interpretation; limits.

## Evaluation cautions
Image generation is stochastic. A single successful sample is not proof. Avoid cherry-picking. Blind or independent ratings are preferable when feasible. Track cost and user consent. Do not upload sensitive or unlicensed imagery.
