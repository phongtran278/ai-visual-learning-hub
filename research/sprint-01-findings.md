## Study design next
Recruit working designers; collect baseline brief/image attempts; run structured lessons; assess pre/post using the same rubric and comparable briefs; include independent raters when possible; report failures, model versions, and selection bias.

## Source quality distinctions
UNESCO and NIST are broad frameworks, not vendor approval. Vendor docs describe product behavior and may change. Human-AI interaction studies support design rationale but cannot directly prove transfer to image generation. A publisher abstract alone is not a full critical appraisal.
