## Required metadata for each source
ID; title; author/organization; URL/DOI; publication/update date; access date; type; applicable model/version; supported claim; limitations; review status; reviewer.

## Rules
- A vendor guide supports claims about its documented product, not universal cross-model effectiveness.
- A scholarly paper does not automatically validate an educational intervention.
- Do not copy large portions of third-party texts into this repository; link and paraphrase with attribution.
- Mark dead or superseded links and retain change history.
