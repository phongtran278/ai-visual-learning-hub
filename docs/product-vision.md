# Product Vision — v0.1

**Status:** Draft · **Date:** 2026-10-21

## Mission
Help designers develop transferable, assessable skills for directing, evaluating, and iterating AI-generated visuals. Nonprofit, learning-first, evidence-informed.

## Audience
Initially graphic, social, marketing and junior art-direction designers. No promise of universal model mastery or certification by AI vendors.

## Principles
1. Learning outcomes before generation infrastructure.
2. Distinguish scholarly evidence, vendor documentation, Hub experiments, and hypotheses.
3. Version claims by platform/model and review date.
4. Learners use their own AI products; the Hub does not ask for passwords or session cookies.
5. Privacy by default; no learner images in the public repository.
6. Publish corrections and uncertainty.

## North-star outcome
Learners can reliably turn a brief into a visual, diagnose mismatches, revise with intention, and explain decisions.

## Out of scope (initial)
Built-in image generation, cross-provider paid API orchestration, automated AI grading, accreditation claims, and public learner portfolios by default.
