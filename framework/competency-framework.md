# AI Visual Competency Framework — Research Review v0.2
**Status:** evidence-informed draft, NOT a standardized/certified competency instrument.
**Reviewed:** 2026-10-21

## Core competencies eligible for pilot
- **AV-01 Visual brief translation:** express audience, purpose, subject, constraints and acceptance criteria. Support: Anthropic/OpenAI guidance on success criteria and evaluation.
- **AV-02 Composition and spatial control:** specify and critique framing, hierarchy, camera, perspective, negative space. Support: Google image guidance; requires design-theory validation.
- **AV-03 Subject and character direction:** count, pose, interaction, visual consistency. Support: image generation/editing workflows; needs cross-model experiments.
- **AV-04 Light, material and style:** describe and evaluate lighting and material appearance. Support: Google image guidance; needs visual design literature.
- **AV-05 Reference and edit literacy:** choose references, describe edits, understand edit-selection limitations. Support: OpenAI and Google image documentation.
- **AV-06 Critique and iteration:** define failure conditions, compare revisions, preserve constraints, record model/version. Support: Anthropic/OpenAI evaluation guidance; human-AI interaction research indirectly.
- **AV-08 Responsible practice:** consent, provenance, rights awareness, limitations and disclosure. Support: UNESCO and NIST.

## Provisional, not yet core
- **AV-07 Production readiness:** professional export, resolution, handoff and brand deployment. Valuable, but evidence and scope need further industry research.

## Assessment
Use task-specific observable rubrics; retain artifacts and revision history. Scores are local feedback, not validated mastery certificates.

## Progression
Execute → Explain → Diagnose → Transfer remains a Hub **hypothesis**. UNESCO uses Understand → Apply → Create for school students; do not claim equivalence.
