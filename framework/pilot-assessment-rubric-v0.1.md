# Pilot Assessment Rubric v0.1

**Provisional formative rubric. Not psychometrically validated and not issued by UNESCO.**

## General scoring
- **0 — Not demonstrated:** no usable evidence or major unaddressed mismatch.
- **1 — Partially demonstrated:** some correct evidence, but important gaps or unexplained decisions.
- **2 — Demonstrated for this task:** evidence satisfies the stated observable criterion with documented reasoning.

Score **per competency and per stage**; do not calculate a universal "AI mastery" score. If model limitations prevent fair testing, mark **N/A**, record why, and offer an alternative task.

| Domain | Understand — check | Apply — check | Create — check |
|---|---|---|---|
| AV-01 | Identifies audience, intent, constraints, acceptance test | Rewrites a brief with testable constraints | Resolves competing requirements and explains rationale |
| AV-02 | Annotates hierarchy, framing, negative space | Meets predeclared composition constraints | Proposes alternatives and defends novel composition |
| AV-03 | Identifies count, poses and interactions | Meets count/pose criteria or documents model failure and corrective attempts | Directs roles/spacing on an unfamiliar brief |
| AV-04 | Correctly identifies light and material cues | Matches agreed light/style attributes | Produces coherent treatment across related assets |
| AV-05 | Distinguishes reference, transformation, edit and copying | Documents intended and unintended changes | Plans and justifies multi-step reference/edit process |
| AV-06 | Identifies measurable failures | Shows targeted before/after correction | Transfers critique process and states stopping rule |
| AV-08 | Identifies consent, provenance and safety concerns | Completes a provenance/risk log | Defends mitigation choices under competing constraints |

## Assessor instructions
1. Review the **brief and acceptance criteria before the final image**.
2. Score task evidence, **not personal aesthetic preference alone**.
3. Record the exact criterion missed and supporting artifact.
4. Note tool/model version, assistance, and possible platform failures.
5. Where possible, blind assessors to learner identity and compare ratings.
6. Disagreements trigger rubric clarification, not silent score averaging.

## Record template
Learner alias | Module | Date | Tool/model/version | Brief ID | Stage | Criterion | Score 0/1/2/N/A | Evidence path | Feedback | Assessor | Caveats

## No certification
These are pilot performance descriptors. No pass/fail thresholds or claims of validated proficiency until empirical testing.
