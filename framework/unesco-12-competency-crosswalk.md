## How to read this crosswalk

- **Original:** UNESCO's named competency block (not a Hub invention).
- **Alignment:** **Direct principle** = Hub explicitly teaches the relevant underlying principle; **Partial adaptation** = related practice but different scope; **Gap** = UNESCO capability is not taught or assessed by current Hub; **Not equivalent** = tempting but inaccurate equivalence.
- **Hub IDs:** AV-01 Brief; AV-02 Composition; AV-03 Character; AV-04 Lighting/style; AV-05 Reference/edit; AV-06 Critique/iteration; AV-07 Production (provisional); AV-08 Responsible practice.
- UNESCO's 12 blocks are a **4 × 3 matrix**. The Hub's seven pilot domains are **not** seven of UNESCO's twelve blocks. Mapping is many-to-many.

## Hub competencies → objectives → assessments

All thresholds below are **pilot proposals**, not thresholds set by UNESCO.

| Hub competency | Understand objective and evidence | Apply objective and evidence | Create objective and evidence | Primary UNESCO anchor |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| **AV-01 Brief translation** | Explain audience, intent and constraints; annotated brief | Turn supplied brief into measurable acceptance checklist | Independently reconcile competing requirements for a new campaign; rationale + brief | Human agency; Problem scoping (partial) |
| **AV-02 Composition** | Explain hierarchy, framing, negative space; annotated visual | Meet placement/text-safe-area criteria; generated artifact + rubric | Propose and justify a new composition under constraints; alternatives and trade-offs | Application skills (specialization) |
| **AV-03 Character direction** | Recognize count/pose/interaction requirements; checklist | Revise a multi-subject scene to satisfy count/pose; before/after | Direct a new ensemble with roles and spacing; revision log | Application skills (specialization) |

## Proposed assessment model

1. **Unit of assessment:** competency × level × task; record model/version, date, brief, artifacts, prompt history, assistance and assessor.
2. **Anchors:** 0 = not demonstrated, 1 = partial, 2 = demonstrated; require **observable artifacts** and written reasoning. No universal pass mark.
3. **Progression:** Understand → Apply → Create is a helpful scaffold, **not a mandatory lockstep sequence**. A learner may have different levels in different domains.
4. **Pilot validation:** test with designers, repeat across briefs, assess agreement between independent raters, publish failure cases and revise criteria.
5. **No certification:** avoid "UNESCO certified", "industry-standard proficiency", and claims that one image proves mastery.

## Scope decisions

**Inherited:** UNESCO's 4 dimensions, the 3 level labels, human agency, responsibility, ethical reasoning, and competency-based assessment orientation.  
**Adapted:** observable design tasks, visual prompting/editing, art direction and iterative critique.  
**Not inherited / gaps:** AI tool creation, AI system architecture and broad civic citizenship outcomes; these must not be presented as covered.  
**Added from other sources:** composition, photography/lighting, visual production, image model behavior and platform-specific capabilities; see the [Source Registry](../research/source-registry.md) and [Evidence Matrix](../evidence/evidence-matrix.md).
