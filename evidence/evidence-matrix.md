## Not yet substantiated
- Four-level Hub mastery scale as a validated psychometric measure.
- Any claim that a specific prompt formula consistently outperforms others across vendors.
- Fixed learning duration or 20-lesson ordering.
- Automated scoring as objective truth.
- "Master" certification or endorsement by OpenAI, Google, Anthropic, UNESCO or NIST.

## Publication gates
A lesson can enter a pilot if it has (1) specific observable outcome, (2) primary source or explicit theory-based rationale, (3) task and rubric, (4) limitations/model applicability, (5) reviewer approval. Mark lessons "pilot / provisional" until evaluated with learners.
